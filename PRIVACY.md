# Privacy Policy – Honest Cookies

_Last updated: 8 October 2026_

Honest Cookies is published by Honest Tools.

**We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.**

- The extension reads and changes cookies only in your own browser, and only on sites you have allowed. Cookies are never sent to us or anyone else.
- The extension makes no network requests of its own. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising and no remotely hosted code.
- The extension does not read the content of the pages you visit.
- Exports are copied to your clipboard or saved as a file on your own computer, and nowhere else. Imports are read from text you paste.
- The only setting it remembers (whether to show cookies for "this page" or the "whole site") is stored in your own browser.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the change is released, and the change will be clearly explained in the store listing.

Questions: please open an issue on this project's GitHub page.
